Keys

The three physical keys. Click Record key, then press any key to bind it. Tap ⌘ / ⇧ / ⌥ / ⌃ on its own for a dedicated modifier key, or hold a modifier and press a key for a combo like ⌘C (combos are experimental). Or expand media control for volume, play/pause, etc.

Rotary dial

What the dial does when pushed in or turned. Media controls (volume, track skip) are the usual choice, but you can record a keyboard key too.

LED

#00ff00

Breathing is hardware-confirmed. The other light modes were recovered from the app's effect map (written to frame byte 30) but are experimental — try them with the keypad connected and check the result. Colour Mode (rainbow/hue), Speed, Trigger Event, Fn layers and per-key lighting still need protocol capture.

Say hello to your Unmand Keypad — Sprint Mode: On. ⚡
The racing theme is a nod to our move to sprint-style working this year. 🏁
This configurator makes it yours: remap the three keys, set what the dial does, and dial in your LED glow. Takes about a minute — here’s how:

  1. Connect — plug in your keypad, click Connect keypad, and approve the browser prompt.
  2. Bind each control — click Record key and press the key you want (hold ⌘ / ⇧ / ⌥ / ⌃ for a modifier), or open Or assign a media control to pick volume, play/pause, etc.
  3. Write it — click Apply all + Save to store everything on the device so it survives unplugging.

Requires Chrome or Edge served over localhost or HTTPS. Reopen this guide any time from ? Guide in the top bar.
